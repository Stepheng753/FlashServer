This Privacy Policy explains how Crossroads Tutoring and developer tools hosted at dev.stepheng753.com ("we", "us", or "our") handle user data when you authenticate and use our Google Cloud API integration. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data accessed through Google APIs.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies strictly to the Crossroads Tutoring administrative, billing, and scheduling software operated by Stephen Giang. The application is an internal productivity tool designed to streamline weekly lesson tracking, invoice preparation, and administrative notifications.

2. Google User Data Accessed & Permissions

Our application requests access to specific Google user data strictly necessary to execute its primary billing and calendar calculation functions. We request the following Google OAuth 2.0 scopes:

📅 Google Calendar (Read-Only)

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly

Purpose: To query calendar events on the designated tutoring calendar, calculate student lesson hours during the billing week, and filter events that start within the target billing window. We do not edit, delete, or create calendar events.

📁 Google Drive

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive

Purpose: To copy the weekly billing spreadsheet template into the designated yearly archive folder in Google Drive and manage spreadsheet file naming for administrative review.

📊 Google Sheets

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets

Purpose: To read student roster names and previous unpaid balances, write calculated tutoring hours into student rows, and update payment status records.

✉️ Gmail (Send)

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send

Purpose: To automatically send a weekly summary notification email containing billing metrics and spreadsheet links directly to the administrator (stepheng753@gmail.com). We do not read, access, or delete your emails.

3. How We Use Google User Data

The data retrieved via Google APIs is used exclusively for:

  • Calculating tutoring session durations for each enrolled student during the active billing cycle.
  • Populating weekly invoice spreadsheets with lesson hours, rates, and rollover balances.
  • Sending automated administrative calculation summary alerts to the developer/operator.
🔒 Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

The Crossroads Tutoring application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing, Selling, and Disclosure

We hold a strict policy regarding the privacy of your data:

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize Google user data or personal data under any circumstances.
  • No Third-Party Transfer: We do not transfer or share your Google user data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used to serve targeted advertisements, marketing campaigns, or retargeting pixels.
  • No AI/Machine Learning Model Training: Google user data is not used to train, retrain, or improve generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

5. Data Storage & Security

Our application runs on a private Linux server. OAuth authentication tokens are stored securely in encrypted or restricted-access local files on the server and are refreshed automatically via HTTPS directly with Google's authentication servers. No personal Google user data is sent to external databases, analytics trackers, or cloud storage providers outside Google Cloud infrastructure.

6. Data Retention & Revocation

You can revoke our application's access to your Google Account at any time. To revoke permissions:

  1. Visit your Google Account Third-Party Apps Permissions page.
  2. Select the Crossroads Tutoring (or Stephen Giang) application.
  3. Click Remove Access (or Delete all connections).

Upon revocation, our application will immediately lose all ability to access your Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, and Gmail APIs.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our software or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised effective date.
